# Great Courses

A curated directory of full-length university lecture series, high-impact MOOCs, and open audio-visual courses that offer deep intuition and rigorous historical analysis across the modern era.

## Revolutions, State-Building, and Democratic Governance

- [The French Revolution (Coursera / University of Melbourne / Prof. Peter McPhee)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/french-revolution)
- [The American Revolution (Open Yale Courses / HIST 116 / Prof. Joanne Freeman)](https://oyc.yale.edu/history/hist-116)
- [The Civil War and Reconstruction Era, 1845–1877 (Open Yale Courses / HIST 119 / Prof. David Blight)](https://oyc.yale.edu/history/hist-119)
- [European Civilization, 1648–1945 (Open Yale Courses / HIST 202 / Prof. John Merriman)](https://oyc.yale.edu/history/hist-202)
